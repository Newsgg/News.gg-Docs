---

Inviting [News.gg](http://News.gg) to your Discord server is quick and easy:

1. **Click the Invite Link** – Use the official [Invite Link](#) to begin.
2. **Select Your Server** – Choose the Discord server where you want to add [News.gg](http://News.gg).
3. **Authorize Permissions** – Review and approve the required permissions.
4. **Complete the Setup** – Once added, follow the setup prompts or use `/help` to view all commands.
